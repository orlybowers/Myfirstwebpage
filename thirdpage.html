Genesis Adoption

Integration

By 2021, Bitcoin entered a phase of unprecedented institutional adoption and geopolitical relevance. Major corporations like MicroStrategy and Tesla added Bitcoin to their corporate balance sheets, while El Salvador made history by becoming the first country to adopt Bitcoin as legal tender. The market reached consecutive all-time highs later that year, driven by the launch of the first U.S. futures-based ETFs. Even as the market experienced a sharp "crypto winter" in 2022 following the collapse of major industry players like FTX, the network's core protocol remained secure and operational.

The ultimate institutional validation arrived in early 2024, when the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission approved the first spot Bitcoin ETFs, opening the floodgates for trillions of dollars in traditional institutional capital. Combined with the completion of its fourth halving event in April 2024, which structurally reduced the daily supply of new coins, Bitcoin solidified its status as a permanent fixture in global finance. Leading into 2026, the conversation has fully shifted from whether Bitcoin will survive to how it will be integrated into sovereign reserves and traditional banking systems worldwide.
